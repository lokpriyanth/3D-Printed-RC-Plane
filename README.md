# 3D-Printed-RC-Plane
A completely original 3D modelled RC Plane design by me. I am also trying to optimize the way it is put together, so yeah, that's about it.

So, if you are actually gonna build it like me these are the things you are gonna want to do yourself- making a small cutout for your servo in the airfoil. The reason I didn't initially add that is cause, you can also fly it with it hanging, taped to the bottom or top side of the wing. It is all about how much refinement you want. I have shared all the files you need to print and make the plane a functional piece.

# Things you need to buy to fly this:-
  1) A 1200kv or a 1400kv bldc motor
  2) A 2200mah 3S lipo battery
  3) SG90 servo motor (x3)
  4) Jumper wires
  5) 30A ESC
  6) A transmitter and receiver (could come in a set) of your choice
  7) Servo tester(optional)

I have made it pretty simple when it comes to printing and assembling the plane, so I hope it doesn't require much explanation.

*Bonus- This plane can fly without a flight controller, but it all depends on the pilot. Nevertheless you will definitely enjoy flying the plane raw(trust me).*

Hope your plane flies. Bye
